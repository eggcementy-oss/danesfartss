The Process
From Dane. To You.
I

The Harvest

Dane sources each emission from a curated diet of artisanal foods. Timing is everything. Quality cannot be rushed.

II

The Capture

Each fart is delicately captured and funneled into a sterilized mason jar using our proprietary BrewSeal™ technique.

III

Jars are allowed to age at room temperature for 6–48 hours, developing full character and complexity of nose.
